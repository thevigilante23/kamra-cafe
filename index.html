OUR STORY

From 1965 to KAMRA

Hi, I’m Nisar, Director of the MRA Group.

Many people have asked us, “What is KAMRA?”

The story goes back to 1965, when my father started MRA. The name KAMRA comes from my elder brother Mohammed, known to everyone as Kunhammad — Kunhammad MRA, or KAMRA.

What began as a bakery in 1965 gradually grew into the restaurant business. We then introduced MRA Express, bringing together authentic Malabar snacks and great tea.

Today, KAMRA is our fourth venture — a café built around Continental food, good coffee, and a welcoming space for people to come together.

Through KAMRA, our aim is simple: to serve great food and create a place where families and friends can eat, connect, work, study, and unwind.
